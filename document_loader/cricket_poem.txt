Cricket lights shine bright tonight,
The bat meets ball with pure delight.
The bowler runs, the crowd roars loud,
A six flies high above the crowd.

The fielders chase across the ground,
While cheers and claps are all around.
Each single run, each wicket taken,
Creates a moment to be awakened.

Through sweat and skill, through joy and pain,
Players fight through sun and rain.
For every match, both brave and true,
The love of cricket pulls them through.

So raise the bat and let it fly,
Chase every run beneath the sky.
For cricket is more than just a game,
It brings us passion, dreams, and fame.
